1. Input: Reads the rental start hour (0–23) and end hour (1–24) using a Scanner.

2. Validation: Checks if the inputs are within range and that start < end. If invalid, prints an error and stops execution.

3. Calculation: Loops through each hour of the rental period to calculate totalCost based on hourly rates:

12 AM – 7 AM & 9 PM – 12 AM: 500 RWF / hour

7 AM – 2 PM & 7 PM – 9 PM: 1,000 RWF / hour

2 PM – 7 PM: 1,500 RWF / hour

4. Output: Prints total rental duration (end - start) and totalCost.

5. Cleanup: Closes the Scanner to free system resources.
