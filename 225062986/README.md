# Individual Lab 1

Reg Number:225062986

## About this project

This project has two small Java programs.

BicycleRental.java works out how much you pay to rent a bicycle.

MushroomGuesser.java guesses a mushroom from your answers.

## Program 1: Bicycle Rental

You type the hour you start (0 to 23) and the hour you stop (1 to 24). Use whole numbers only.

Price for each hour:

0 to 7 and 21 to 24 costs 500 RWF.
7 to 14 and 19 to 21 costs 1000 RWF.
14 to 19 costs 1500 RWF.

Example:

Start hour: 6
End hour: 8
You pay: 1500 RWF

Hour 6 costs 500 and hour 7 costs 1000, so the total is 1500.

## Program 2: Mushroom Guesser

Think of one of these mushrooms: Agaric Jaunissant, Amanite tue-mouche, Cepe de bordeaux, Coprin chevelu, Girolle, Pied bleu.

The program asks at most 3 questions. Type 1 for yes and 2 for no.


Example:


Does it grow in a forest?

Does it have a convex cup? 2

Does it have gills? 2
